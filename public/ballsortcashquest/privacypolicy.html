Privacy Policy

BallSortCashQuest

Last updated: July 3, 2026

BallSortCashQuest ("the App") is a free puzzle game developed by an independent developer. This Privacy Policy describes how the App handles your data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Game progress — coins, highest level reached, and ad statistics are stored on your device and synced to the cloud using an anonymous account (no email or personal identifier required).
  • Country code — detected from your device's SIM/network settings. Used only for withdrawal request processing.
  • Litecoin address — provided by you only when submitting a withdrawal request. Not collected automatically.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To save and restore your game progress across devices
  • To process withdrawal requests for earned rewards
  • To serve advertisements and measure their effectiveness
  • To improve the App and fix technical issues

3. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google) — cloud storage, anonymous authentication, analytics, and crash reporting.
    Firebase Privacy Policy
  • IronSource LevelPlay — ad mediation platform for banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads.
    IronSource Privacy Policy
  • CPX Research — optional surveys you can choose to complete for in-app rewards.
    CPX Research Privacy Policy

4. Advertising

The App may display advertisements served by third-party ad networks. These networks may use device identifiers, IP addresses, and interaction data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard security practices to protect your data. Game data is stored securely in Firebase Firestore. No passwords, financial information, or sensitive personal data are collected by the App directly.

7. Your Rights

  • You may request deletion of your data by contacting us
  • You may stop data collection by uninstalling the App
  • You may opt out of personalized ads via your device settings

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
